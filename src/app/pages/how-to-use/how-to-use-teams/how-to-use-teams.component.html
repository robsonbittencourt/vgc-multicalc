The Teams page lists Pokémon Champions tournament teams with full sets, Stat Points and replica codes. The teams come from VGCPastes and from players who submit their own.

Filters

  • Regulation, Team, Creator and Event: find the teams of a Regulation, a team name, a player or a tournament.
  • Source: All, VGCPastes or Community, for the teams submitted here.
  • Placement: All, Top 8 or Winners.
  • Replica Code and SPs: only the teams that have a replica code or full Stat Points.
  • Pokémon: Add Pokémon to find the teams that use them.
  • Compact and Detailed: one line per team, or the full sets of every team.

The filters open in Filters, and Recent Events is after the list.

Next to the list, Recent Events, Recent Teams and Most Viewed Teams are shortcuts to the latest and most viewed teams.

Use a Team

Copy To: My team loads the team as one of your teams, Opponents loads it as the opponent list.

Tap a team to open its Paste. Open in Calc as loads it as My team, one of your teams, or as Opponents, the opponent list.

Paste opens the team as a Paste in a new tab, and the copy button copies its Showdown text.

Opponents loads the team as the opponent list of Team vs Many and Many vs Team.

Submit a Team

Submit TeamSubmit adds your own team to the list, credited to you. Paste the Showdown text and fill in the team name, the creator, your Twitter, the event, the placement and the replica code, and choose SP or EV for the spreads. Teams are moderated and can be removed without notice.
