Type Calc shows the Offensive Coverage and the Defensive Coverage of your whole team, so you can see where it is strong and where it is exposed.

Against Another Team

Unlike a plain type chart, the calc uses the moves and abilities of your team. It updates as soon as you change a Pokémon or a move.

Second team: hold Ctrl (Cmd on Mac) and click another team in the Teams widget. The tables then show your Pokémon against the Pokémon of that team, instead of against each type.

Second team: in the Teams tab, drag another team onto your team. The tables then show your Pokémon against the Pokémon of that team, instead of against each type.

Abilities and items that change type matchups are counted, like Levitate, Air Balloon, Scrappy, Mold Breaker, Pixilate and Freeze-Dry against Water.

Offensive Coverage

Shows how your team hits each type:

  • Super Effective: which types are weak to at least one move of your team.
  • Each Pokémon: the best effectiveness of the moves of each of your Pokémon against each type.
  • Gaps: the types your team cannot hit for neutral or super effective damage.

Defensive Coverage

Shows how each type hits your team:

  • Weaknesses: which types deal super effective damage to each of your Pokémon.
  • Resistances and Immunities: which types your Pokémon can safely switch into.
  • Total Weak and Total Resist: how many of your Pokémon are weak to or resist each type, to spot shared weaknesses.

Insights

A summary of the coverage, in the Insights tab:

  • Offensive: your two Pokémon with the most super effective hits, and the two with the most not very effective ones.
  • Offensive types: the type your team hits super effectively the most, and the one it hits not very effectively the most.
  • Defensive: your two Pokémon with the most resistances and immunities, and the two with the most weaknesses.
  • Defensive types: the type your team resists the most, and the one it is weak to the most.
