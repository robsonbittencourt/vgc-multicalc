Export your sets in Showdown format, share your team as a Paste, or fill in the Team List for a tournament.

Export a Single Pokémon

Export above the build gives the Showdown text of the Pokémon you are editing, with its moves, item, ability, Nature and SPs. It works for your team and for the opponents.

Export a Full Team and Create a Paste

Export Paste in the Teams widget opens the Create Paste page with the Showdown text of the whole team. Copy the text, or create a Paste: a page with the sprites, items, moves and spreads of the team, shared by a link. Create Paste in the header opens the same page.

Export Paste and Team List are in the Teams tab, and Create Paste is in the menu.

  • Team name and Replica Code: shown on the Paste.
  • Stat points: SP or EV, the unit of the spreads in the text and in the Paste.
  • Show SPs: turn it off to share the team without the spreads.
  • Password: only people with the password can open the Paste. A lost password cannot be recovered.

Anyone who opens a Paste can load the team into the calc, as their own team or as opponents.

Team List

Team List in the Teams widget fills in the official team list of a tournament as a PDF. Add your player data and age division, and choose the pages: Staff, OTS (Open Team Sheet) or Both. All fields are optional.

Export Opponents and Calcs

Export in the opponents header gives the Showdown text of every Pokémon in the list, to share a list of threats or a piece of the meta.

Export Calcs gives the calc description of every card, one per line, ready to paste anywhere.

Export and Export Calcs are under Management, after the list of cards.
