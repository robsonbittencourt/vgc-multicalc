Team vs Many calcs one of your Pokémon against a whole list of opponents at the same time. Use it to see what your attacker OHKOs or 2HKOs across the format, and which targets it cannot break.

Pick the attacker in Your Team. Every opponent below gets a card with the damage of your move and the KO chance.

Pick the attacker in the sprite tabs at the top. The opponents come after the build, one card each, with the damage of your move and the KO chance.

Every card updates: change a move, an item, the SPs or the field and the whole list is recalculated.

Combined Damage

To calc two of your Pokémon attacking the same target, hold Ctrl (Cmd on Mac) and click the second Pokémon, or use Combine Damage. Every card then shows the damage of both attacks together.

To calc two of your Pokémon attacking the same target, hold the second Pokémon in the sprite tabs. Every card then shows the damage of both attacks together.

Core Features

  • Opponents: choose My List for the Pokémon you added, Meta for the most used Pokémon of the current Regulation, or one of your teams. Changes to Meta Pokémon are lost when the page reloads.
  • Order by Damage: sorts the cards from the most damage to the least, so the KOs come first.
  • Best Move: uses, against each opponent, the move of your attacker that deals the most damage.
  • Roll Level: Low, Medium or High picks the roll used by every card.
  • Filters: Pokémon and Set show only the opponents you are looking for.
  • Def and SpD: apply a stat boost or drop to every opponent at once.
  • Export Calcs: copies the calc description of every card.
  • Open a card: click a card to edit the build of that opponent.

Opponents, the filters, the boosts and Export Calcs are after the list of cards. Roll Level, Order by Damage and Best Move are in the Modifiers tab.

The SP Optimizer in KO mode finds the fewest SPs to KO the opponents. See the SP Optimizer page.

Layout and View

Next to the Opponent Defenders title, Stacked puts the opponents below your team and Side by Side puts your team on the left and the opponents on the right, so you can see the damage change while you edit your Pokémon. In Side by Side, Roll Level, Order by Damage, Best Move and the boosts are under Options.

Cards shows one card per opponent. Dense groups the opponents in OHKO, 2HKO, 3HKO and 4HKO+, from the most damage to the least, with the damage under each Pokémon. Hover a Pokémon to see its item, nature, ability and SPs.

They are next to the Opponents title. Hold a Pokémon in Dense to see its item, nature, ability and SPs.
