Probability Calc shows how reliable your team is in battle. It looks at the accuracy of your moves and at the chance of common game events, so you can choose your team and your moves knowing the risks.

Consistency Score

The Consistency Score measures how reliable your team is based on the accuracy of its moves. Each Pokémon gets a score from 0 to 100, and the scores are combined into the score of the team.

Compare teams: the score is made to compare teams. A higher score means a team that performs the same way more often, which helps you choose between two versions of a team.

Moves with 100% accuracy raise the score and less accurate moves lower it. A Pokémon with several inaccurate moves gets an extra penalty, because its whole moveset becomes less dependable. A single very inaccurate move can lower the score a lot, as it can cost you a match.

Move Probabilities

For each move of your team, a table shows the chance of success over several turns:

  • Hit and miss chances
  • Chances of secondary effects
  • Chances over several turns
  • Spread moves and single-target moves

Abilities and items that change accuracy, like Compound Eyes or Wide Lens, are counted.

Combined Probability

Combined Probability and the reference tables are in the General tab.

Type up to five chances and choose in Expected what you want: At least one, All or None of them happening. The Result shows the combined chance.

Field

The weather and Gravity change the accuracy of moves: Thunder and Hurricane never miss in Rain and drop to 50% in Sun, Blizzard never misses in Snow, and Gravity raises the accuracy of every move.

Reference Tables

The calc also has tables for common game events:

  • Critical hits
  • Protect used in a row
  • Turns to sleep and waking up
  • Fully paralyzed
  • Freeze
  • Snapping out of confusion
  • Multi-hit moves

They help you plan when to use a move or an ability, knowing how likely each event is.
