Many vs Team turns Team vs Many around: a whole list of attackers against one of your Pokémon. Use it to see what your Pokémon survives in the format and which threats are the most dangerous to it.

Pick the defender in Your Team. Every attacker below gets a card with the damage it deals to your Pokémon and the KO chance.

Pick the defender in the sprite tabs at the top. The attackers come after the build, one card each, with the damage it deals to your Pokémon and the KO chance.

The cards are the same as in Team vs Many with the sides swapped: the attacker is on the left and your Pokémon is the one on the nameplate.

Every card updates: change an item, the SPs or the field and the whole list is recalculated.

Combined Attackers

Drag a card by its handle onto another to calc both attackers hitting your Pokémon in the same turn. The card then shows the damage of both attacks together, and Separate splits them again.

Core Features

  • Opponents: choose My List for the Pokémon you added, Meta for the most used Pokémon of the current Regulation, or one of your teams. Changes to Meta Pokémon are lost when the page reloads.
  • Order by Damage: sorts the cards from the most damage to the least, so the biggest threats come first.
  • Best Move: uses, for each attacker, the move that deals the most damage to your Pokémon.
  • Roll Level: Low, Medium or High picks the roll used by every card.
  • Filters: Pokémon and Set show only the attackers you are looking for.
  • Atk and SpA: apply a stat boost or drop to every attacker at once.
  • Export Calcs: copies the calc description of every card.
  • Open a card: click a card to edit the build of that attacker.

Opponents, the filters, the boosts and Export Calcs are after the list of cards. Roll Level, Order by Damage and Best Move are in the Modifiers tab.

The SP Optimizer in Survive mode finds the fewest SPs for your Pokémon to survive the attackers in the list. See the SP Optimizer page.
