Bring your Pokémon and teams into the calc instead of typing every set. The calcs then use the exact sets you play.

Supported Formats

Paste a PokePaste link, a VR Pastes link or the Showdown text of a set or a team. The calc detects the format. A link also brings the title of the paste as the team name.

Use SP or EV at the bottom of the window to say if the spreads in the text are Stat Points or EVs.

Import a Single Pokémon

In One vs One, Import above each build replaces the Pokémon of that side. In Team vs Many and Many vs Team, Import above the build adds a Pokémon to your team while it has fewer than six.

Import a Full Team

Import in the Teams widget loads a whole team, with its moves, items and SPs, into a new team.

The Teams widget is the Teams tab.

Import Opponents

In Team vs Many and Many vs Team, choose My List in Opponents and click Import to add Pokémon to the list. You can import as many as you want, to test your team against the whole format or against a list of threats.

Opponents and Import are under Management, after the list of cards.

From a Paste or the Teams Page

A Paste and every team on the Teams page can be loaded straight into the calc, as My team or as Opponents. See the Teams page and Exporting & Sharing.
