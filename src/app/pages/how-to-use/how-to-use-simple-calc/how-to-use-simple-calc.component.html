One vs One is the classic damage calc: two Pokémon attacking each other. You see how much damage the Pokémon on the left deals to the one on the right, and how much it takes back.

Side by Side

Each side has its own build and its own moves. The damage of the four moves of each Pokémon is shown at the top, next to its nameplate.

The two Pokémon are the sprites at the top. Tap one to see its build, its moves and the damage it deals.

Both sides update together: change a move, an item or a stat on one side and the damage of both sides is recalculated.

Damage and Rolls

  • Rolls: the 16 damage rolls of the selected move, and the calc description, ready to copy.
  • KO Chance: the chance to OHKO, 2HKO and beyond, counting HP, items like Sitrus Berry and end of turn damage.
  • Roll Level: Low, Medium or High picks the roll used by the HP bar and the damage results.
  • Times Used in a Row: for moves that change stats on every use, like Draco Meteor or Overheat, choose how many times in a row the move is used. The KO chance counts every use with the stat changes of the uses before it.

Build

Every detail of both Pokémon can be changed in the build:

  • Stat Points: spread up to 66 SPs, at most 32 in one stat. The final stats update as you type.
  • Nature and Mod.: pick the Nature and the stat boosts or drops of each stat.
  • Abilities and Items: triggers like Choice Specs, Life Orb or Multiscale are applied to the damage.
  • Mega Evolution: click the Mega Stone next to the item to Mega Evolve and to go back.
  • Status: Burn, Paralysis and the other conditions that change the damage.
  • Save set: saves the build under a name. The set shows up under the Pokémon in the Pokémon list.

Below the stats, the SP Optimizer finds the fewest SPs to survive the other side or to KO it. See the SP Optimizer page.

Field

The field applies to the calc of both sides. Weather, terrain, Gravity and the rooms are under Both. Screens, Helping Hand, Tailwind and the other side conditions are under Left and Right.

The field and Roll Level are in the Modifiers tab.
