Speed Calc shows where your Pokémon stands in the turn order against the Pokémon of the format, your opponents or one of your teams. Turn order decides many matches, and Speed is where most spreads start.

Speed List

The list puts every Pokémon on a Speed scale, from the slowest to the fastest, with your Pokémon in it. Under each sprite you see where the number comes from: Min, Max, a Nature, the share of players that use that Speed, Scarf for a Choice Scarf spread or Booster for Booster Energy. Speed Tie marks two Pokémon with the same Speed.

Effective Speed: the calc applies abilities like Swift Swim in Rain, items like Choice Scarf, Paralysis and the field, and shows the number used in battle, not only the stat.

Click a Pokémon in the list and Outspeed sets the fewest Speed SPs your Pokémon needs to move before it.

Filters

The filters are in the Modifiers tab.

  • Filter: Reg M-C shows the Pokémon of the Regulation, Opponents shows your opponent list, or pick one of your teams.
  • Top Usage: with a Regulation, how many of its most used Pokémon are in the list.
  • Mode: which Speeds show up for each Pokémon: Stats and Meta, Stats, Meta or Base.
  • Pokémon: search one Pokémon to compare your Speed with all of its spreads.
  • My Whole Team: puts every Pokémon of your team in the list, not only the one you are editing.

Speed Modifiers

Two places change the Speeds in the list: Opponent side, only for the opponents, and the Field, for both sides. Your Pokémon also follows its own build.

Opponent side is at the top right of the Speed List. is in the Modifiers tab. It changes the Speed of every opponent in the list:

  • Speed Modifier: a Speed boost or drop.
  • Icy Wind: sets the Speed Modifier to -1, as after Icy Wind or Electroweb.
  • Paralyzed: halves the Speed.
  • Choice Scarf: calcs the opponents holding Choice Scarf. Mega Pokémon keep their Mega Stone. With it off, the Regulation list already shows a Scarf spread, with its share of players, for the Pokémon that are used with the item.

Field

The Field of the Speed Calc only has what changes Speed, for your Pokémon and for the opponents. It is below the Teams widget. It is in the Modifiers tab, below Opponent side.

  • Tailwind: doubles the Speed. Under Attacker it is for your Pokémon, under Defender for the opponents.
  • Trick Room: reverses the list, so the slowest Pokémon move first.
  • Weather and Electric Terrain: turn on Speed abilities like Swift Swim in Rain, Chlorophyll in Sun, Sand Rush, Slush Rush and Surge Surfer.

Speed Insights

Click a Pokémon in the list to see its insights. They are in the Insights tab.

  • Range: base Speed, the range with a neutral Nature, the top with a positive Nature and 32 SPs, and, for Trick Room Pokémon, the bottom with a negative Nature and 0 SPs.
  • Most used Speeds: the Speeds players use most, and how many of the same Pokémon each one outspeeds or ties with.
  • Speed items: the Speed it reaches with Choice Scarf, or with Booster Energy for Paradox Pokémon, and how many players use the item.
  • Source: the statistics come from the Pokémon Showdown Bo3 ladder, across all ratings.
